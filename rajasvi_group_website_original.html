ABOUT RAJASVI GROUP

Engineering Experience. Built Into Every Project.

Rajasvi Group is an emerging engineering and construction company providing integrated project solutions across industrial, infrastructure, marine and civil construction sectors.

Established in 2021, the Group combines the capabilities of an evolving organisation with more than 30 years of practical industry experience across engineering, fabrication, construction, marine operations and workforce management.

Our capabilities extend from structural steel fabrication and piping systems to marine engineering, civil construction, industrial maintenance and manpower deployment.
